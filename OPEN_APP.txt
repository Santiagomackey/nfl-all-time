Open the app with either option:

Option 1:
- Double-click `teams/NFLHOMEPAGE_fixed_grid.html`

Option 2:
- Double-click `start_nfl_all_time.bat`
- This opens:
  http://127.0.0.1:8765/teams/NFLHOMEPAGE_fixed_grid.html

If the launcher does not work:
- make sure Node.js is installed
- or double-click `run_server_here.bat` and keep that window open while you use the site
